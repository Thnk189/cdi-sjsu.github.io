We Are Hiring Officers!

The CDI team is looking for active indivduals that can help grow this club in the community and events we do. We have three main branches that we will be testing this semester that control our non-existent Standard Operation Procedures

The Executive Branch

Handles high-level strategic planning, club paperwork, forms, and team management. Oversees either the Community or Projects sub-branch and reports directly in weekly meetings with the President.

Secretary / Executive Officer: Manages paperwork, tracks meeting updates, and keeps club operations running smoothly.

The Project Branch

Drives technical execution, workshop events, and club design initiatives—including our main semester project, a TinyTapeout CPU.

Events Manager: Handles event scoping, budgeting, demo prep, logistics, hardware setups, and live demo assistance during workshops.
Project Manager: Manages sub-teams, regulates design workflows, and ensures design milestones are met for our TinyTapeout CPU submission.

The Community Branch

Drives member interaction, social media, visual content creation, and funding efforts across three sub-teams:

📢 Community Engagement

Branch Lead: Overlooks sub-teams and relays weekly progress updates.
Social Media Manager: Builds post schedules and manages social platform activity.
Discord Community Lead: Moderates the server, manages channels, and posts announcements.

🎨 Media Production

Media Production Lead: Coordinates designers and photographers on schedule.
Graphic Designer: Creates posters, event flyers, web graphics, and social media visuals.
Photographer / Videographer: Captures photos/videos at workshops and events.

💡 Fundraising

Director of Fundraising: Sets financial goals and leads club funding strategy.
Campaign Planner: Organizes campus fundraising events.
Grants / Sponsorship Coordinator: Researches grants, drafts applications, and manages sponsor outreach.
