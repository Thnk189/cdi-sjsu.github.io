Chip Design Initiative

Open-source RTL and silicon design for students.

CDI is a student-run SJSU engineering club for learning the digital design flow: idea, RTL, simulation, synthesis, silicon submission, and board bring-up.

The club is centered around TinyTapeout, Verilog HDL, open-source EDA tools, FPGA experiments, and making hardware projects less scary to start.

Want to Join? Browse Resources

What we do

Student hardware ideas become working digital designs.

Members bring small logic projects, PSoC demos, FPGA/Vivado experiments, and TinyTapeout-ready ideas into a shared club workflow.

We write readable RTL, run simulations, review waveforms, document projects, and help each other get unstuck before the design gets too mysterious.
